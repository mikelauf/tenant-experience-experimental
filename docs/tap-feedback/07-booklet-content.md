## Conflicts to confirm with Spencer
- **The Sandbox:** the page headline says 300 guests, but the body says "up to 400 guests". The site uses 300.
- **Sky Bar:** the headline says 80 guests, but the body says "up to 75". Core's record says 75. The site uses 80.
- **Redwood Park:** the booklet says 1,500 guests, while earlier sources and Core say 1,000. The site uses 1,500.
- **Contact email:** the booklet lists `pyramid.events@yoda.com.cy`. The `.com.cy` domain (Cyprus) looks like an error. **Not published.**
- "The Sanbox" is misspelled on p17 of the booklet. The site uses "The Sandbox".
